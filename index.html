Our Philosophy
A great building is an act of will. We begin with the question, not the answer.

Founded in 1990 in Stockholm, UA Arch has grown from a small structural consultancy into an integrated architecture and civil engineering practice with studios in Stockholm, Hotline, and Email.

We believe that the best built environments emerge from the dialogue between disciplines — where architects, structural engineers, and urban planners work from the same table from day one. Our integrated model eliminates the translation errors that sink projects and add cost.

Every engagement begins with a rigorous site and programme analysis. We ask the questions most firms defer: what does this place need to be in 50 years? How does the ground behave? What load does the community ask of this building?

Work with us
I
Structural Honesty
We expose our reasoning at every stage. Clients see the thinking behind every load calculation and every design decision.
II
Integrated Practice
Architecture, structural, and civil disciplines share studios and project teams — not handoffs and RFIs.
III
Long-horizon Thinking
We design for the fifth decade, not the opening ceremony. Material choices, maintenance access, and adaptive reuse are first-order concerns.
IV
Precision Over Volume
We accept fewer commissions than we receive. Each project deserves the full attention of a dedicated senior team.
